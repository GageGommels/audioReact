audioReact

Visuals that move to whatever your microphone hears. Put on some music, start it up, then cast this tab to your TV.

Audio is analysed on this device only. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Getting it on your TV

Chromecast / Google TV (Chrome or Edge)
Open the browser menu (⋮), choose Cast, save and share → Cast…, pick your TV, and keep the source set to Tab. Then press F for fullscreen.
Apple TV / AirPlay TV (Mac, iPhone, iPad)
Open Control Centre, choose Screen Mirroring, pick your TV, then go fullscreen here.
HDMI or a smart TV browser
Plug in a cable and go fullscreen, or open this page directly in the TV's browser if the TV has a microphone.

The microphone that listens is on this device, so keep it within earshot of the speakers. Controls hide after a few seconds without movement.
